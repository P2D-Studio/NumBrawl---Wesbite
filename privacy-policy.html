4. Game history and account deletion

If you request account deletion, we will delete or de-identify personal account information associated with your account, subject to applicable legal requirements and any limited retention exceptions described below. To preserve the integrity of past multiplayer matches, certain historical game records may remain associated with a non-reusable player identifier. That retained identifier will not be linked to your email address or made available for another person to use as an account. Historical records may continue to appear to players who participated in those matches.

We will not retain information in identifiable form for longer than necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, except where retention is required or permitted by law. See our Account Deletion page for instructions.
