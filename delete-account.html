ACCOUNT DELETION.

How to request deletion of your Numbrawl account and associated personal information.

Important: Account deletion removes personal account details. Certain historical match records may be retained in a non-reusable, non-email-linked form so other players can continue to see their past game history.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to support@numbrawl.com with the subject “Numbrawl Account Deletion Request”.
  2. Include the email address or account identifier associated with your Numbrawl account so we can locate it. Do not send your password.
  3. We may contact you to verify that you control the account before processing the request.
  4. Once verified, we will delete the personal account details associated with the account, subject to applicable legal requirements and limited retention exceptions.
  5. We will send a confirmation email when the request has been processed. You can then uninstall the Numbrawl app from your device.

What happens to past game records?

To preserve the integrity and history of multiplayer matches, game records such as past match results and ratings may remain stored against a non-reusable player identifier. This identifier will not be linked to your email address or made available for use by another account. This allows people who previously played against you to continue seeing the relevant historical match records.

What information is deleted?

Personal account details associated with your account will be deleted or de-identified, subject to legal retention requirements. The exact categories depend on the information collected by the released version of the app. Please see the Privacy Policy for more information.
